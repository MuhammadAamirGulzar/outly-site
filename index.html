Outly

Find the people worth writing to.

Outly runs on your own computer. It finds the people a campaign is about, reads what is public about them, ranks the fit, and drafts a personal message to each one — which you read before anything leaves.

What it does

  • Academic — researchers whose published work fits yours, through OpenAlex, their papers and their own pages.
  • Business — companies and their owners or directors, from OpenStreetMap, Google Maps and the web, then their own websites.
  • Job search — the companies advertising the work you do, from free job boards, one city a day if you are travelling.
  • Anything else — describe who you want to reach in your own words.

It never sends without you

Every campaign starts in manual mode: the app opens a compose window and you press Send. If you connect Gmail, it can instead put a batch into your Gmail drafts, or send a batch you have approved, under a daily cap. A message edited after you approved it is refused rather than sent, and sending by SMTP is not built at all.

Where your things live

On your computer, in one SQLite file beside the app, together with your profile and your keys. Nothing is uploaded to us — there is no “us”: no server, no account, no analytics. See the privacy policy for what the app talks to and why.

Running it

It is a Python app you run locally: install it, then outly web opens it in your browser at 127.0.0.1:8765. It works with no API keys at all; free keys add reach, and it stops at each free allowance rather than spending money.
