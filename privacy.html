Outly

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 September 2026.

Outly is a program you install and run on your own computer. It has no server, no user accounts and no analytics. Nothing you do in it is sent to the developer, and there is no infrastructure that could receive it.

What the app stores, and where

All of it stays on your computer, in the folder you installed it in:

  • your profile — your name, contact details, publications, projects and signature;
  • the contacts a campaign found, what was publicly read about each one, the drafts written for them, and what you recorded about each conversation;
  • any API keys you chose to add, in a local .env file;
  • if you connect Gmail, the resulting Google credential, in a local file.

The developer cannot see any of it. Deleting the folder deletes everything the app has.

What the app talks to

Only services you have chosen to use, and only to do the thing you asked for: OpenAlex, ORCID and Crossref for research records; OpenStreetMap and, if you add a key, Google Maps or Google Places for organisations; free job boards for open roles; the web-search provider whose key you added; an AI provider you chose, to write and rank drafts; and the public websites of the organisations in your campaign. Each request identifies the app and, where you set one, a contact address for you.

Google account data

Connecting Gmail is optional, and the app works fully without it. If you connect it, Outly asks for the narrowest permissions that do the job:

  • Create and manage drafts (gmail.compose) — to put the messages you approved into your own Gmail drafts, so you can read them there and press Send yourself.
  • Send (gmail.send) — only if you choose the approved-send mode, and only for a batch you approved, under a daily cap.
  • Message metadata (gmail.metadata) — labels and headers only, never message contents, for the conversations the app itself created. This is what lets it notice that you sent a draft, that someone replied, or that a message bounced, instead of asking you to record each one by hand.

That data is used for those purposes only. It is processed on your own computer, stored in your own local database, and never transmitted to the developer, never sold, never shared with anyone, and never used for advertising or to train any model. You can disconnect at any time in the app's Settings page, which deletes the stored credential, and you can revoke the app's access from your Google account permissions.

Limited Use. Outly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other people's data

A campaign collects information that its subjects have published about themselves — a paper, a company website, a public profile page, a job advert. The app records where each item came from, never guesses an email address, never infers anything about who a person is, and refuses an audience defined by nationality, ethnicity or religion. It does not fetch LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram or Pinterest. As the person running it, you are the one responsible for what you collect and what you send.
